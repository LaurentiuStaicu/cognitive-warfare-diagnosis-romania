## About the project

**Diagnosis of Cognitive Warfare in Romania** is an interdisciplinary monograph and interactive web application devoted to analysing the structural vulnerabilities of Romanian society and the mechanisms through which Russian information operations and Putinist ideology exploit and amplify them.

The study proposes an explanatory architecture built around three interdependent layers — institutional, neurocognitive, and cultural — and traces the causal connections between macro-institutional conditions, individual processes of judgement and decision-making, and collective outcomes. The diagnosis is followed by a sequential protocol for rebuilding cognitive and institutional resilience.

This repository contains the English-language edition for English-speaking readers. The interactive application turns the monograph into a digital environment for reading and analytical exploration: readers can follow the academic text, consult executive summaries, examine diagrams and tables, and navigate the glossary and bibliography.

## Structure of the interactive web application

### 1. Editorial cover — monograph synthesis and interactive causal plate

The landing page introduces the subject, title, and subtitle of the study, together with its three-layer explanatory architecture:

- **Layer A — macro-institutional, historical, and sociological**;
- **Layer B — neurocognitive, semiotic, and cybernetic**;
- **Layer C — cultural unconscious and group psychodynamics**.

The cover features an interactive 2D vector synoptic plate built around **The Macro–Micro–Macro Model**. Its inspector enables readers to explore five components: `Layer A`, `Layer B`, `Moral Filter`, `Layer C`, and the `Chapter 6 Protocol`.

The landing page also provides access to the audiovisual synthesis published on YouTube, quick sharing through **Facebook**, **X**, and **WhatsApp**, and a link-copying function.

### 2. Monograph walkthrough — full text and executive summary

The reading path comprises **19 sections**: the Introduction and the 18 subsections of Chapters 1–6, from `1.1` to `6.4`.

Readers can choose between two reading registers:

- **Full Academic Text** — the complete argument, including theoretical development, explanatory mechanisms, and bibliographic references;
- **Executive Summary** — a condensed presentation of the diagnosed mechanisms, systemic vulnerabilities, and implications for institutional decision-makers and public policy.

Reading tools allow users to switch between `Text + Registers`, `Text Only`, and `Registers Only`, activate `Focused Reading`, and dynamically filter content by Layers A, B, and C. The text is supplemented by **39 analytical cards** from the *Theoretical Register* and *Empirical Reality*, **183 interactive academic citations**, **236 author anchors** connected to the lateral bibliographic dossier, and **55 contextual terminological explanations** (*tooltips*).

### 3. Laboratory of the seven 2D vector diagrams

The laboratory brings together the monograph's **Figures 1–7**, reproduced as SVG vector graphics and available both in their corresponding thematic sections and in a dedicated exploration space.

The diagrams represent causal, institutional, cybernetic, and behavioural models. Depending on the figure, interactive exploration enables readers to:

- examine nodes and relationships between components;
- follow causal loops and the sequences connecting mechanisms;
- switch between the represented regimes or scenarios;
- consult explanations associated with diagram elements.

### 4. Synthesis tables, institutional responsibility matrix, and regional control group

This section connects the diagnosis of vulnerabilities with the reconstruction protocol presented in Chapter 6. It includes:

- **Table 1 — Synthesis of the diagnostic causal architecture**, organised around three layers and five major dimensions of societal vulnerability;
- **Table 2 — Sequential reconstruction protocol**, structured around three intervention phases and the four determinants of the Lalonde–Dever model;
- **Institutional Responsibility Matrix**, assigning actions across the three sequential phases and relating interventions to public institutions, regulatory authorities, educational actors, and relevant civil-society structures;
- **Regional Control Group**, comparing Romania with Estonia, Poland, and the Republic of Moldova.

### 5. Glossary, bibliography, and monograph search

The conceptual and bibliographic apparatus can be consulted separately and remains connected to the monograph text:

- **Interdisciplinary Glossary — 55 key concepts**, filterable by Layers A, B, and C and by polarity;
- **Complete Bibliography — 152 works**, alphabetised in accordance with APA 7th Edition and filterable across five scientific domains;
- **Monograph Search**, providing deterministic search across the full text, glossary, and bibliography.

The glossary provides access to explanations of concepts in their analytical context, while the bibliographic dossier helps readers identify the works and authors cited throughout the monograph.

## The argumentative thread

The argument follows a causal and operational sequence:

1. **Diagnosis of endogenous vulnerabilities** in Romanian society across the model's three Macro–Micro–Macro layers.
2. **Analysis of the mechanisms that exploit and amplify those vulnerabilities** through information operations and ideology.
3. **Explanation of cognitive capture** and its effects on collective orientation, judgement, and decision-making.
4. **Assessment of societal consequences**, from polarisation to the erosion of resilience and collective self-sabotage.
5. **Reconstruction of cognitive and institutional resilience** through the proposed sequential protocol.

The Christian-secular axiological filter, the OODA loop, and the regional comparison support this explanatory structure. They are not isolated topics, but components connecting diagnosis, mechanisms, and intervention.
